02 — Our Story
A COLONY OF BREWERS
We started in a garage off State Street in 2019 with a propane burner, a stockpot, and a stubborn belief that great beer belongs to everyone. Utah is the Beehive State — built on the idea that small, relentless work adds up to something big.
Today we brew a mile above sea level, where thin air and Wasatch snowmelt make for a crisp, clean character you can't fake. Every batch is a little colder, a little brighter, and 100% made by hand.

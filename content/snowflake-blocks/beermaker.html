The early days

1996

It started with a single taproom in Heber Valley and one stubborn idea: that the high country deserved beer built for it. Cold water, clean air, six thousand feet of altitude.
Thirty years on, we still brew where we started — east of the range, between Park City and Heber. The geography is the recipe, and it hasn't moved.

The early days

Wasatch Back was founded in 1996, when a pair of homebrewers moved their kettles up into the high valleys east of the range and opened a small taproom in Heber Valley. The first batch was a clean amber poured for a room of skiers and ranch hands. Thirty years on we still brew at altitude, on the same water, for the same kind of room.

The Wasatch Back.

East of the Wasatch range, in the high valleys between Park City and Heber. Cold water, clean air, six thousand feet of altitude. The geography is the recipe. We've been brewing it since the first taproom opened in Heber Valley.
